Frequently Asked Questions

How do we obtain our Texas marriage license?

Purchase your license from any Texas County Clerk at least 72 hours before your ceremony (and no more than 90 days before). I provide guidance on specific county clerk procedures.

Do you attend the wedding rehearsal?

Rehearsal attendance is included in the Signature Package and can be added to the Custom Celebration package for $175.

How far in advance should we reserve our date?

Most couples book 6 to 9 months in advance, especially for popular spring and fall weekend dates.

How is the marriage license handled after the ceremony?

I review all signatures immediately following the ceremony and personally mail the completed license to the issuing county clerk via certified tracking within 48 business hours.
